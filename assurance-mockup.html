    What each row is

    One row per case. These are records a hospital already holds: the AI's output and the clinician's conclusion.

    ColumnWhat goes in it
    system_idstroke, chest, sepsis, triage or assistant
    dateDate of the case, as YYYY-MM-DD
    ai_output1 if the AI flagged the case, 0 if not. For the assistant, the word summary
    clinician_finding1 if the clinician confirmed the condition, 0 if not. For the assistant, the reviewer's verdict: ok, minor_error or major_error
    age_group18-39, 40-64 or 65+
    sexF or M
    nationality_groupUAE national, Arab expatriate, South Asian expatriate, or Other expatriate

    How the figures are calculated

    Sensitivity: of the cases the clinician confirmed, the share the AI flagged. Missed cases lower it.

    Specificity: of the cases without the condition, the share the AI correctly left alone. False alarms lower it.

    Major error rate: for the record assistant, the share of reviewed summaries with an error a clinician judged could change care.

    Each weekly point uses the four weeks up to that week, so one quiet week does not cause a false alarm. The first three points use fewer weeks.

    A system needs action when a figure is below its agreed floor. It is on watch when a figure is within 2 points of the floor, or when one patient group is 5 or more points below the other patients, the difference is too large to be chance (a standard two-proportion test at the 99% level, which is strict because many groups are checked at once), and the group has at least 30 confirmed cases.

    Floors are set by the hospital for each system. The ones here are examples.
