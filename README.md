# Sentinel demo

A working demo of Sentinel, which lets a hospital check every AI system it uses against its own clinicians' findings, on its own patients, and keeps the records Abu Dhabi's Department of Health requires.

Live: https://arshinsikka.github.io/sentinel-assurance/

- Start with the 3-minute guided tour, or explore on your own.
- Every figure is calculated in the browser from a sample file of made-up cases (what each AI system said, and what the clinician later concluded). Nothing is sent anywhere.
- "Where it fails" breaks missed cases down by patient group, scanner and case type, and only calls something a pattern when it passes a fixed statistical rule.
- Download the sample file from "About this demo", edit it, and load it back to see every page recalculate.
- Risk scoring follows the Department of Health Abu Dhabi Responsible AI Standard and Risk Management Protocol (October 2025), including the Protocol's risk rating table.

Example General Hospital is fictional, vendor names are placeholders, and all data, including the vendor-reported figures, is made up.
